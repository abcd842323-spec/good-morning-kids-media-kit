About the Brand

Good Morning Kids helps Indian parents of 3–5 year olds teach through play — bilingual rhymes, morning routines, printable worksheets and simple daily tools, in Hindi and English side by side.

What we make

Nursery-rhyme Shorts in Hindi and English, a blog for parents, printable activity packs, 29 free online tools (name tracing, flashcards, reward charts, colouring pages and everyday family calculators), and the Hindi Seekho app for early Hindi learning.

Why brands work with us

A focused, hard-to-reach audience: parents actively looking for learning products for their toddler right now. Every channel speaks to the same parent, so one collaboration can appear as a Reel, a Short, a blog review and a pin — all from a single brief.

Our Audience

Indian parents of toddlers aged 3–5, following along in Hindi and English. They come for rhymes and routines, and stay for printables, tools and product ideas for their child.
